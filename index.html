Comprehensive Utility Suite

Everything You Need to Lift with Absolute Confidence

We replaced bulky manuals and complex spreadsheets with an intuitive interface. Access seven robust engineering modules right in the field.

1. Centralized Configuration Hub

Say goodbye to scattered notes. Safely record, edit, and instantly recall specific crane capacities, structural measurements, and load matrices for repeated operations.

2. Dynamic Rating Encyclopedia

Instant access to a massive database of wire rope slings, synthetic webbing, and chain configurations. Seamlessly toggle between Imperial and Metric standards.

3. Augmented Angle Camera

Utilize your device's camera to verify rigging geometry visually. Overlay smart protractors on your load to ensure your sling angles stay within safe hoisting tolerances.

4. Automated Stress Analysis

Input your payload weight and sling geometry to instantly compute individual leg tension. Automatically verify if your rigging setup meets the required safety factor.

5. Advanced Lift Simulation

Digitally recreate the site environment. Configure boom length up to 200m, adjust building clearance obstacles, and test your hook positioning safely before the actual lift.

6. Ground Bearing Safety

Prevent catastrophic ground failure. Calculate outrigger forces and input Soil Bearing Capacity (SBC) to automatically determine the exact support pad dimensions required.

7. Instant PDF Lifting Plans

Turn your calculations into professional documentation instantly. Compile simulation telemetry, sling limits, and soil pressure data into a neat, print-ready PDF Lifting Plan. Speed up approval times with your site managers and safety officers.
