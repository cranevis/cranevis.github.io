Cranevis Application Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Cranevis ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices when you use the Cranevis mobile application ("App"). We have designed this application from the ground up with a strict privacy-first approach.

1. Information Collection and Use

We explicitly do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage telemetry, or identifiable information. All operations, mathematical calculations, and parameter inputs (such as load weights, crane dimensions, and sling specifications) are processed entirely locally on your device's hardware.

2. Device Permissions and Local Storage

The App may request minimal device permissions strictly necessary for its core functionality. Specifically, we may request access to your device's local storage solely to allow you to save your generated Lifting Plan PDF reports or preserve your application preferences. This data remains encrypted and isolated on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

Cranevis operates as a 100% offline application. We do not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), advertising networks, or external SDKs that track user behavior. Your interactions within the app remain completely private and unmonitored.

4. Data Security

Because our application does not connect to the internet to transmit your data, the risk of external data breaches or unauthorized server access is virtually non-existent. We rely on the native security frameworks provided by the Android operating system to keep your locally stored application data safe.

5. Children's Privacy

Cranevis is a professional utility application designed for adult industry professionals, such as riggers and safety engineers. It is not intended for, nor do we knowingly collect data from, children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected directly within the application and on this page, indicated by an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or technical inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our local data processing methods, please reach out to our support team via the developer email address provided on our official Google Play Store listing.
