Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 09, 2026

Thank you for using our app. This Privacy Policy explains how we manage and protect your privacy when you use this application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal information. This app functions entirely by processing the numbers and parameters you input locally on your device to perform calculations. None of the data you enter is ever transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

2. Device Permissions

This app may require certain device permissions solely to execute technical functions locally on your device (such as local storage to save your application preferences or previous calculation configurations). All such data remains securely on your own device.

3. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate or connect with any third-party services, analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or external SDKs that could track your activities or collect data to identify you.

4. Security

We value your trust. Because this app does not request or transfer any personal data (such as names, emails, phone numbers, or location data), the risk of data leaks through this application is non-existent. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage or local device processing is 100% impenetrable.

5. Changes to Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

Engineered for Modern Lift Professionals

Cranevis packs eight robust modules into one seamless interface, replacing heavy books and complex spreadsheets right in the field.

1. Data Center

Central storage for your lifting configurations. Safely record, edit, and instantly recall specific crane capacities, building measurements, and load matrices.

2. Sling Table

A dynamic rating encyclopedia covering wire rope slings, synthetic webbing, and chain configurations. Supports multi-grade selections with a seamless imperial-metric toggle.

3. Angle Camera

Use your phone’s camera system to lock in sling orientation angles. Snap a quick layout photo and overlay measuring protractors to assure dynamic hoisting tolerances.

4. Sling Tension

Enter load specs and apex geometries to compute individual line stress. Instantly verify if the sling tension stays safely inside operational limits.

5. Simulation Workspace

Configure telescopic or lattice boom models. Adjust surrounding structural boundaries and dimensions dynamically, scaling loads on-the-fly to test clearances.

6. Ground Bearing Pressure

Analyze ground outrigger forces in real time. Input Ground Bearing Capacity (GBC) guidelines to compute the necessary support pad areas, ensuring total structural safety.

7. Material Weight

Calculate the total weight of any object simply by entering its physical dimensions and selecting the material type. Instantly outputs accurate mass estimations for lifting plans.

8. Professional Lifting Plan Builder

Compile all your simulation telemetry, sling designs, and soil pressures into an instantly generated, print-ready, professional PDF Lifting Plan. Ready to share with your safety officers or print on-site.
